
- Introduced **2004** in public areas (libraries, parks, transit hubs) as **ATM-style terminals** for controlled intranet access.
- Equipped with **keyboard, display, and roller-ball mouse**; built for durability and public use.
- Designed under a **centralized internet paradigm**, limiting personal connectivity.
- Ran curated portals: municipal services, news, academic databases, and local intranet directories.
- Continuously upgraded through the 2010s–2020s; never replaced due to modular, iconic design.
- Featured **HarDis ports** for uploading, downloading, and routing data, enabling operation under fractured networks.
- Late-2020s replacement attempts failed; terminals remained integral to infrastructure.
- Post-Ashfall, surviving units serve as **relays, archives, and settlement-controlled access points**.
