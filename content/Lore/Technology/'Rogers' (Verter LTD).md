In 2008, a technology slowly and silently replaced the underperforming flip phones of the early 2000's era. Even if clearly inspired by the previous design, one crucial difference set it apart from the rest of the competition:

- **_It was a hybrid, between the classic clamshell cellular phones, and a brand new generation of pagers, marking the "new era of personal & peer-to-peer communication" via "Rogers."_**

While not widely accepted in the beginning, "Rogers" soon became a staple, going so far as to become the second most sold communicator worldwide in the mid-2010s, while being constantly improved upon, even through the 2020s.

It can be described as a thicker version of the flip phone, with the main screen flipping out horizontally instead of vertically, and the pager part being the bottom-most compartment.

In-game they are used as a tool to explore your quick access inventory and checking back quest objectives on the main screen, while the pager part is used for Time/Date, as well as receiving quest-important queues and remote messages.

Most "Rogers" currently found have been manufactured by a company called "Verter" (Verter LTD), as indicated on their backplate.
