
-  Skyscrapers, reaching upwards of 20 average stories in height; stabilized through the use of central concrete cores with *tuned mass damper* mechanisms.
-  Core directly linked to the [[Elevated Highway]] via high-tensile cables, to provide active support to the roadway in the case of extreme weather conditions or seismic activity.
-  Partially inhabitable, but mostly reserved for hotels and offices, with immense sightseeing platforms on the rooftop, partially accessible to the public.
-  Most of the Pylons integrate into the interconnected grid of above-ground passageways present in Vancouver, while being themselves interconnected.
