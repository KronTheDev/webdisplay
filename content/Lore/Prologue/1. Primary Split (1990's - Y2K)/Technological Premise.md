In this reality, numerous aspects of technology have been neglected: Left out to be re-discovered later, after a singular event shattered technological progress in the eyes of the world — the ***Y2K***. In this timeline, the much feared electronics collapse was not over-estimated by public opinion:

- ***Interbank settlement failures.***
    
- ***ATM outages across regions.***
    
- ***Stock markets halt repeatedly.***
    
- ***Insurance systems fail to reconcile data.***
    
- ***Logistics software desyncs supply chains.***


Safe to say — ***The 2008 collapse from our time, came early in this reality***.

This, in terms, destroyed the momentum of newly rising technologies: people building deep distrust and resentment in anything "hi-tech" produced post-Y2K. Especially, the domain of communication, the Internet, and quite a bit of other upcoming technological marvels were now under the watchful and judging eye of the population.

And so they stagnated: Instead of growing into brand new products, old designs were kept and slowly improved upon as time went on. Even when the crisis was already far behind, in the early 2020s, the trend still followed suit with a wave of new products, among which were the new versions of the [['Rogers' (Verter LTD)]], as well as the brand new [['HarDis' (Advanced Floppy Disks)]].
