Due to the Y2K event, technology and advancement were greatly distrusted by the general population, which led to newer technologies being largely unadopted. Following this, pushes for tradition and the past dominated in the 21st Century. Technology and Architecture stayed roughly static in the 2010s style for the 2020s and 2030s, up until 2035 when a new abundance of resources (primarily oil in Canada) pushed people to develop further. This ended only 4 years later because of the A-Day event on 7/5/39, so much of the more revolutionary technology is untested, in early production, or in rare supply. 

Prior to 2035, people began fearing a Resource War, so they stockpiled various supplies in stockpile bunkers, much like the bunkers people had in the late 1900s. Governments also stockpiled oil, seeds, freshwater, ammunition, guns, etc. in underground stockpile centers. They were placed under major cities, with the intention for government officials to use them as a way to control large areas. While these resources would be extremely vital following A-Day, they are also extremely hard to reach due to the sulfur clouds. Also, bringing up tens of thousands of pounds of resources is not realistic when the very air is deadly.

**2000:** Y2K event - Brief collapse in infrastructure that left people destablized and untrusting of governmental and technological innovations.
**Pre-2035:** Roughly static technology and architecture. Things advanced to, at most, 2010s technology and architecture IRL and stayed there.
**2035:** New resource deposits, primarily oil in Canada.
**Post-2035:** New innovations, not yet widely produced and distributed.
**2039 / A-Day / Labor Day / 5th of September:** Yellowstone supervolcano explodes.

### Recap Notes
- General distrust of the population in change, be it technological ([[Technological Premise]]), social ([[Sociological Premise]]) or political.
-  Reflects in the architecture as well: The buildings are stuck in a 2010-ish, semi-brutalist style, reminiscent of the Vancouver Law Courts (more will be in Lore/World/Architecture)
-  Due to the general sense of distrust, internal insecurity has began building up as well, and this reflects in the creation in late 2020s of various stockpile centers as the fear of potential resource wars began grinding on the people's mentality.
-  But the scare was beginning to clear up since 2035, as brand new deposits of fossil resources have been found  at the far north of Canada, and kickstarted a brand new technological revolution, breaking from the decades of fear and embracing opportunity.
-  Unfortunately, this feeling is short-lived, as A-Day struck only 4 years later on Labor Day, 2039.
