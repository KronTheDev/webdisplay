## Dialogue State Flow Outline

Player–settlement interaction follows a structured dialogue state system designed to support branching outcomes while remaining manageable for implementation.

### Core Dialogue States

**1. First Contact**

Triggered when the player approaches or enters a settlement for the first time.

Possible states:
- Suspicion
- Neutral curiosity
- Immediate hostility (rare)

Typical outcomes:
- Limited conversation
- Restricted movement within settlement
- Initial quest or proof-of-trust requirement

---

**2. Conditional Interaction**

Once the player has passed the initial contact phase, dialogue options expand.

Possible states:
- Neutral
- Cooperative
- Distrustful

Settlement NPCs may provide:
- small barter options
- local information
- basic tasks

---

**3. Established Relationship**

Triggered once the player gains sufficient trust.

Settlement behavior changes:
- broader dialogue options
- access to traders
- access to internal areas
- higher value quests

---

**4. Hostile State**

Triggered if the player breaks settlement rules or harms members.

Possible consequences:
- dialogue locked
- guards hostile
- settlement access denied

Hostility may be temporary or permanent depending on severity.
