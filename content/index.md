### Post-Ashfall Narrative Survival

The eruption did not end the world.  
It changed the terms of survival.

Set in a city divided by elevation and atmosphere, ***Above the Line*** explores resilience, infrastructure decay, and the cost of comfort in a fractured society. Progress is not guaranteed. Safety is temporary. Movement is a choice.

This project is developed through a structured, phase-based production model prioritizing stability, clarity, and deliberate growth.

This site documents the world, systems, and roadmap behind the work.

Build carefully. Move forward.
