*After several months of unsettling seismic swarms and anomalous geological shifts in the Colorado region, the chain of events was officially dubbed the MSE-38, or Montana Seismic Events, following the year 2038. That year marked the first time in North American history where more people died from seismic-related events in the central U.S. than from all other natural disasters combined.*

*The quakes themselves were rarely catastrophic alone — most registering between 5.0 and 6.8 on the Richter scale — but their frequency, depth, and unusual spread left scientists increasingly worried. Entire neighborhoods sank into sinkholes. Highways cracked open. Water tables shifted. Sulfuric fogs began to appear across valleys. Geysers vanished or exploded violently. And yet, the events were disconnected, or so it seemed.*
