
- Above ground, primarily at the height of 8 - 10 stories (Low Part; Below the Line) and rising in the central part of Vancouver to 11 - 12 stories (High Part; Above the Line).
-  They were built as a solution to the ever-growing traffic on the main highway of Vancouver: ***Hwy 99***, due to the impossibility to expand horizontally (skyscrapers), or dig tunnels (too close to sea).
-  Concrete-reinforced structures, with support pillars extending to the ground; tied with reinforced cables to special adjacent skyscrapers, called [[Pylons]].
-  Due to the main purpose of the highway expansion is to provide an additional fast route *through* the city, without sacrificing on horizontal space: There are no clear ramp-offs from the expansion towards ground level within the Downtown Area, only re-joining the main highway at its extremities.
-  Built in the mid-2020s, along with the Pylon initiative.
