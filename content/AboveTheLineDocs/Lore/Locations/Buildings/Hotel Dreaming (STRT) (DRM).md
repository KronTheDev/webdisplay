## *About:*

*Old and eerily melancholic Building, reminiscent of the 2020's (IC) lateral expansionism era of architecture. Houses the "Dreamers" minor settlement on its two top floors.*

---
#### *Details:*

***State:***

The building is relatively stable, but with visible marks of concrete decay and acid damage towards the Line, and the roof edges.


***Resources:***

**Required:** Low and approaching critical. Crops have mostly failed.
**Practical:** Average.
**Miscellaneous:** Low.
**Junk:** Average.


***Trade (SET):***

Environmentally cut off after a key gangway collapsed. Small delegation (3 ppl) were sent to investigate routes, and never returned.


***Demographics (SET):***

Originally a 24 people settlement, the "Dreamers" (soon linked) lost quite a few members throughout the years. After the unfortunate passing of Old Dale, many left seeking opportunities in the surrounding districts. Some became scavengers, yet most joined the surrounding settlements. Finally, after the recent loss of the 3 member that partook in the delegation, the count ultimately came down to 10.


***Power (SET):***

Two small generators power the main water filters, along with whatever lights may be present inside. Fuel comes from the nearby multi-story Parking LotCrop growth has failed after numerous attempts at trying to power sun-filter lamps.


***Protection (SET):***

Environmental Protection — very few access points.
