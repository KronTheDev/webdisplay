Throughout the documentation and internal design notes, a system of **Name-Tags** is used to simplify references to recurring environmental, gameplay, and lore concepts.

## *IMPORTANT:*
These are **not Obsidian tags**.  
They are short semantic identifiers meant to:

- *Keep documentation concise.*
- *Avoid repeating long terms.*

Name-Tags appear in **uppercase abbreviations**, usually consisting of **2–4 letters**.

### Environmental Context Tags

- **BTL** — *Below the Line*  
  Refers to all gameplay environments located beneath the atmospheric safety threshold where the toxic cloud accumulates. These zones require filtration equipment and expose the player to atmospheric hazards.

- **ATL** — *Above the Line*  
  Refers to areas located above the toxic atmospheric layer. These environments are generally safer and form the primary habitation zones of surviving settlements. The abbreviation strictly refers to the **environmental state**, not the name of the project.

- **AL** — *At the Line*  
  Transitional areas that sit directly at the altitude where the cloud layer stabilizes. These regions often feature fluctuating environmental conditions and partial atmospheric exposure.

- **VF** — *Volcanic Fallout*  
  Ash and particulate matter originating from the eruption events.

---

### Atmospheric Elements

- **CL** — *The Cloud*  
  The large atmospheric mass created after the eruption events. It consists of a dense mixture of volcanic gases and particulate matter that settles at lower altitudes across the region.

- **GS** — *Gas System / Gas Mix*  
  The toxic gas mixture composing the cloud. It includes multiple hazardous components that affect the player differently depending on exposure duration and filtration efficiency.

- **HZ** — *Hazard Zone*  
  Any area where atmospheric conditions present an immediate risk to the player.

- **SL** — *Sulfur Layer*  
  The middle-ground Cloud band where most toxic accumulation occurs.

- **DZ** — *Dead Zone*  
  Regions where survival is nearly impossible due to extremely high gas concentration.


---

### Survival System Tags

- **FI** — *Filter Integrity*  
  Remaining operational condition of the player's mask filter.

- **ET** — *Exposure Time*  
  The accumulated time the player spends exposed to the gas system.

- **OX** — *Oxygen Condition*  
  Refers to breathing stress and oxygen deprivation states.

- **PT** — *Poisoning Threshold*  
  The point at which toxic buildup begins producing severe physiological effects.

---

### Infrastructure / Urban Tags

- **GW** — *Gangways*  
  Elevated pedestrian bridges connecting buildings above street level.

- **PY** — *Pylons*  
  Structural skyscrapers used to stabilize elevated infrastructure.

- **EH** — *Elevated Highway*  
  The reinforced highway system that runs above much of the city.

- **SC** — *Sky Corridors*  
  Interior building connections used for protected movement between structures.

---

### Technology / Devices

- **RG** — *Rogers Communicator*  
  The personal communication and interface device used by the player.

- **HD** — *HarDis*  
  Advanced floppy-style storage disks used for data transfer.

- **IT** — *Intranet Terminal*  
  Public access computer terminals connected to the localized network.

- **RT** — *Relay Tower*  
  Infrastructure used to extend localized intranet signals.

---

### Gameplay / Player State

- **HP** — *Health Condition*  
  The player's physical state.

- **ST** — *Stamina*  
  Physical endurance during movement and exertion.

- **BR** — *Breathing Rate*  
  Audio and visual cues linked to oxygen stress or poisoning.

---

### Social / World Context

- **STL** — *Settlement*  
  Any organized group of survivors occupying a structure.

- **SCV** — *Scavenger*  
  Individuals who search abandoned structures for supplies.

- **RD** — *Raiders*  
  Hostile survivor groups operating within hazardous zones.

- **INR** — *Inners*  
  Survivors trapped below the Line who could not escape to the mountains.

---

### Example Usage

> Settlements located **ATL** rely on elevated infrastructure to remain outside the influence of the **CL**.

> Gangways (**GW**) allow limited travel **ATL**, but most scavenging operations occur **BTL**, where **ET** and **FI** must be carefully managed.
